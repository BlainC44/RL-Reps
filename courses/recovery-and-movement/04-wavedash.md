# Goal

Gain a burst of speed by dodging just as your wheels touch the ground after a small jump or fall.

# Cues

1. Do a **small jump** (a quick tap of jump).
2. In the air, **tilt the nose up slightly**.
3. Right as the **back wheels are about to touch** the ground, **dodge** (press jump with the stick pointed in the direction you want to go).
4. The car should skid forward along the ground with extra speed instead of flipping.

# How to practice

- Start while driving slowly so you can feel the timing.
- If you flip fully, you dodged too early. If nothing happens, you dodged too late.

# A rep counts when…

You did the small jump, tilted back, and tried to time the dodge with the landing.

# You finished the course 🎉

Mix these into your warm-up: two minutes of half-flips and wavedashes before every session.
