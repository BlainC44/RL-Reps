# Goal

Get off the ground faster by using your **second jump** to gain height quickly. This is how you beat opponents to high balls.

# Cues

1. **Hold boost** and **jump**, holding the jump button for a moment.
2. **Tilt back** while you rise.
3. Press **jump again** very quickly, with the stick still tilted back so the car keeps pointing up instead of flipping.
4. Keep boosting and steer toward the ball.

> The key is the second jump with the stick held back. If your car flips forward, you let go of the stick before the second jump.

# How to practice

- **Reps 1–10:** no ball. Just fast aerial straight up and land. Feel the rhythm.
- **Reps 11–30:** fast aerial toward the ball in Free Play.

# A rep counts when…

You did both jumps with boost on and the car stayed pointed upward.
